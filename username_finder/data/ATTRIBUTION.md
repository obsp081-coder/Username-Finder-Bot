# Curated data attribution

These are small reviewed token selections, not comprehensive dictionaries. They convey no Snapchat availability. Each JSON entry has its own source reference; `manifest.json` records versions, licenses, transformations and SHA-256 checksums.

- **Open English WordNet Community, Open English WordNet 2025**, derived from Princeton WordNet. [Downloads](https://en-word.net/downloads), [contributors and source](https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Selection: 110 individually chosen common English lemmas. Changes: select allowlist, lowercase ASCII, retain lexical-entry references; remove other fields. Upstream XML SHA-256 is recorded in the manifest. Attribution and license apply to this subset. No endorsement is implied.
- **U.S. Social Security Administration**, [Top names of 2020–2025](https://www.ssa.gov/oact/babynames/decades/names2020s.html): 60 tokens from ranks 1–30, lowercase. U.S. federal government public data. The bulk ZIP returned 403; the published table was read via the browsing tool and the selected tokens were recorded in `tools/reviewed_source_selection.json`. Its checksum is labeled `selection:` in the manifest, not represented as the original archive checksum. No birth counts or personal records are redistributed.
- **Bayanat Labs, Arabic–Latin Name Variants v0.1.0**, derived from Wikidata, [publisher page](https://www.bayanatlabs.com/open/arabic-latin-name-variants), [data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Bayanat-Labs/arabic-latin-name-variants). Data: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Only five published Latin variants of محمد are included. No page prose is redistributed. The checksum is of the reviewed selection, not of the entire upstream dataset.
- **Gaming**: independent selection of four ordinary gameplay terms, dedicated under CC0 1.0. Per-entry links to Epic Games/Roblox documentation support terminology; no publisher text/artwork or software is copied. No endorsement is implied.

wordfreq is a reviewed potential frequency-ranking source and is **not bundled or used**. Its CC BY-SA data is not mixed into these packages. English-person names are names attested in U.S. births, not a claim that every name has English origin. Arabic variants are a very small subset, not coverage of Arabic names.

Rebuilding is a deliberate development operation: `python tools/prepare_data.py`. It reads the reviewed selections and cached/source OEWN XML, checks membership, and writes packages plus hashes. Runtime searches are offline. Changing any library changes its snapshot; paused jobs cannot silently adopt new content. `runtime/data_evidence` is development evidence, not a runtime dependency or release requirement.
